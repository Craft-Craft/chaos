Privacy Policy of Pretty Cursor

Effective Date: August 2026

Introduction

Pretty Cursor ("we," "our," or "this extension") takes your privacy seriously. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use this browser extension.

Data Collection

Data We Collect

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We only collect the following types of non-personal data, and all data is stored locally on your device:

  1. User Preferences: Your selected cursor style, size, color, and effect toggle settings
  2. Enable State: The extension's enabled/disabled status, to remember your usage habits

How We Collect Data

All data is stored locally on your device through the browser's local storage API (chrome.storage.local / localStorage). We do not:

  • Collect your browsing history
  • Collect your personally identifiable information
  • Transmit data to any server
  • Share any data with third parties
  • Use any analytics or tracking services

Data Usage

We only use the collected data to:

  • Provide and improve extension functionality
  • Remember your preferences
  • Display your chosen cursor style on web pages

All data processing is done locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using the browser's local storage API. You can delete this data at any time through your browser's extension management page.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties. All data remains entirely on your device.

Permission Usage

storage Permission

Used to store your cursor style preferences and feature toggle states locally on your device. Data is never uploaded to any server.

Host Permissions (<all_urls>)

Used to inject content scripts on all web pages to apply your selected cursor style. All processing is done locally and no data is collected or uploaded.

Third-Party Services

This extension does not integrate any third-party analytics services, advertising services, or data collection services.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, data security depends on your device security. We recommend that you:

  • Keep your browser and operating system updated
  • Use strong passwords to protect your device
  • Do not share your device with others

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete the extension and all its data at any time through your browser's extension management page
  • You can clear your browser's local storage data
  • You can uninstall the extension at any time

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be notified through extension updates. We recommend that you review this privacy policy periodically to stay informed.
